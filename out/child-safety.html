1.Our Commitment

Verity (“Verity,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a social networking and knowledge-sharing platform intended for people aged 13 and over.

We do not tolerate child sexual abuse or exploitation (“CSAE”) in any form, anywhere on Verity. This page sets out the standards we apply, how we enforce them, and how to reach us about a child safety concern.

These standards apply to every part of the Verity mobile application and related services, including posts, stories, comments, direct messages, voice and video calls, live streams, debates, expert consultations and career listings.

2.Prohibited Content and Behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited on Verity:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including photographs, videos, drawings, computer-generated or AI-generated imagery, audio and text.
  • Sexualised depictions of minors, including content that presents a minor in a sexual manner or focuses on a minor's body in a sexual way, whether real or fictional.
  • Grooming: contacting a minor with the aim of sexual abuse or exploitation, including building trust for that purpose, requesting intimate images, or arranging to meet.
  • Sextortion: threatening or blackmailing a minor using real or fabricated intimate images.
  • Trafficking: advertising, recruiting or facilitating the sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Facilitation: sharing, requesting, offering or linking to any of the above, whether on Verity or on another service.

Accounts that engage in any of this behaviour are permanently removed from Verity. There are no exceptions and no warnings.

3.Age Requirements

Users must be at least 13 years old to create a Verity account, or older where local law sets a higher minimum age.

Accounts that we become aware belong to children below the minimum age are removed, and any personal information collected from them is deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.

4.How We Enforce These Standards

  • In-app reporting. Every post, comment and profile on Verity can be reported from inside the app using the Report option in its menu. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Removal and account action. Content that violates these standards is removed, and the account responsible is suspended or permanently deleted.
  • Reporting to authorities. Confirmed CSAM is reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement, as required by applicable law. We preserve the evidence needed for those reports.
  • Cooperation with law enforcement. We respond to valid legal requests from law enforcement relating to child safety.

5.How to Report a Concern

If you see content or behaviour on Verity that may harm a child, please report it:

  • In the app: open the menu on the post, comment or profile and choose Report, then pick the reason that best fits. Reports are confidential.
  • By email: write to masresha004@gmail.com. Please include the username, a link or screenshot where possible, and a short description of the concern.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

7.Legal Compliance

Verity complies with applicable child safety laws, including mandatory reporting obligations, and with the Google Play Developer Program's Child Safety Standards policy.

We may update these standards to reflect changes in our Services, our practices or applicable law. The latest version is always available on this page with its revision date.

Need to report a child safety concern?

Use the Report option in the app, or email our child safety contact. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.
