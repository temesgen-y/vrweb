1.Introduction

Welcome to Verity.

Verity (“Verity,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a social networking and knowledge-sharing platform that enables users to connect, communicate, exchange knowledge, engage with communities, consult experts, and discover professional opportunities.

We recognize the importance of your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the Verity mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By using Verity, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it separately.

2.Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Verity may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and Personal Information

When you register or manage your account, we may collect:

  • Full name and username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile picture
  • Password authentication data
  • Date of birth or age-related information, where required
  • Country, language, and preferences
  • Account type (Personal, Creator, or Business)
  • Profile biography and interests

Passwords should be protected using secure authentication mechanisms. We do not require users to provide their passwords to other users.

2.2 User-Generated Content

We process content you create or upload, including:

  • Text posts and comments
  • Photos and videos
  • Stories and other shared media
  • Audio recordings and voice messages
  • Reactions, likes, and shares
  • Community contributions
  • Debate participation
  • Questions submitted to experts
  • Professional endorsements

Content you choose to publish publicly may be visible to other users and may be redistributed by them.

2.3 Communication Information

Verity provides communication features that may include private messaging, voice calls, video calls, and expert consultations.

Depending on the feature and its technical implementation, we may process:

  • Message content
  • Shared attachments
  • Voice messages
  • Call participants
  • Call timestamps and duration
  • Technical connection information
  • Consultation communications

Where end-to-end encryption is implemented, we will describe its scope and limitations accurately in the relevant product information.

We do not claim that all communications are end-to-end encrypted or that calls are never recorded unless those practices have been verified.

2.4 Professional and Expert Information

Users who register as experts or professionals may provide:

  • Professional qualifications
  • Expertise categories
  • Employment history
  • Education
  • Certifications
  • Professional experience
  • Consultation availability
  • Professional endorsements
  • Verification documents, where necessary

Certain professional information may be visible publicly depending on profile settings and platform functionality.

2.5 Technical and Device Information

When you use Verity, we may collect:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • Device or application identifiers
  • IP address
  • Language settings
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Performance and error logs
  • Session and authentication information

Information collected by integrated third-party software development kits (SDKs) must also be reflected in our applicable disclosures.

2.6 Location Information

If Verity offers location-dependent functionality, it may process location information when the user enables relevant features.

This may include approximate location derived from network information or precise location with applicable permissions.

The application will disclose when precise location is collected and explain its purpose.

2.7 Sensitive Information

Some users may voluntarily provide sensitive information when requesting expert advice, including health-related or other personal circumstances.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in public posts.

Where sensitive information is processed, Verity will apply appropriate safeguards and any consent or other legal requirements applicable to that processing.

3.How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  1. Create and manage user accounts.
  2. Authenticate users and protect account security.
  3. Display profiles and user-generated content.
  4. Personalize feeds and content recommendations.
  5. Connect users with experts and communities.
  6. Facilitate messaging, calling, and consultations.
  7. Support expert verification and endorsements.
  8. Operate the Trust Ring reputation system.
  9. Recommend relevant career and professional opportunities.
  10. Respond to support requests.
  11. Detect fraud, spam, harassment, and misuse.
  12. Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our Services.
  13. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or additional notice where required.

4.Public Profiles and Content

Verity is a social networking platform, and certain user information may be publicly visible.

Depending on your account settings and the available functionality, this may include your username, profile image, biography, publicly shared posts, professional information, and visible community contributions.

Users are responsible for selecting appropriate visibility settings and deciding what information to share publicly.

Even if content is subsequently deleted, copies previously saved or shared by other users may remain outside our control.

5.Private Messaging, Calls, and Expert Consultations

Verity enables users to communicate and exchange information.

Private communication content is intended for authorized participants, subject to the actual service architecture, authorized processing, safety operations, and applicable law.

Communication information may be processed to deliver messages, establish calls, prevent abuse, and provide requested consultations.

The availability and scope of encryption, moderation, storage, and recording depend on the implemented feature.

Verity will not represent a communication as end-to-end encrypted unless it is protected by a verified end-to-end encryption implementation.

6.Trust Ring and Reputation System

Verity may use a Trust Ring system designed to support credibility and constructive participation.

Depending on how the system is implemented, reputation indicators may consider:

  • User contributions
  • Community engagement
  • Professional endorsements
  • Account verification
  • Compliance with community guidelines
  • Other relevant participation signals

Trust Ring information may influence how users discover profiles or evaluate credibility.

Reputation scores should not be interpreted as a guarantee of a user's professional competence, honesty, or reliability.

Where applicable law requires additional rights concerning automated decision-making, users may contact Verity using the privacy contact information below.

7.Expert Advice and Health-Related Information

Verity allows users to request assistance from individuals presenting professional knowledge or expertise.

Users may submit questions containing personal or sensitive information.

Information provided during consultations may be shared with the experts selected to participate in the consultation and with necessary service providers.

Expert-generated content is provided by the relevant participant and does not automatically represent professional advice endorsed by Verity.

Unless a specific service expressly states otherwise, Verity is not an emergency response service.

Users experiencing medical emergencies should contact appropriate emergency services.

8.Career and Professional Opportunities

Verity may offer professional networking and career-matching functionality.

Relevant information may include:

  • Professional profiles
  • Work experience
  • Education
  • Skills
  • Career interests
  • Opportunity preferences

Such information may be used to recommend professional opportunities or connect users with relevant recruiters and organizations, subject to applicable visibility controls.

9.Permissions Used by the Android Application

Depending on the features implemented, Verity may request the following Android permissions.

Camera: To capture profile photos, create media content, participate in video calls, or support other camera-enabled features.

Microphone: To record voice messages, participate in audio/video calls, and create audio content.

Photos and Videos: To select, upload, or share media. Access will be limited to the scope of the permissions granted and the features used.

Notifications: To deliver messages, interaction alerts, consultation reminders, and other application notifications.

Location: Where implemented, to support location-based services, subject to applicable permission requirements.

Permissions are requested only when applicable to the implemented functionality.

You can manage permissions through your Android device settings. Restricting permissions may limit certain app features.

10.Sharing of Personal Information

We may disclose relevant information in the following circumstances:

Other Users

Information that you publish or intentionally share may be accessible to other users according to the applicable feature and visibility settings.

Experts and Consultation Participants

Information you provide during a consultation may be made available to the relevant participants.

Service Providers

We may use authorized service providers for services such as hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, storage, communications, and payment processing.

These providers may process data only as appropriate to the services they perform and applicable contractual and legal obligations.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or other legally permitted circumstances, including protecting users and the security of the Services.

Business Transactions

Where permitted by law, data may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable safeguards.

We will disclose any additional data-sharing practices, including advertising-related sharing, if such activities are implemented.

11.Third-Party Services

Verity may integrate external services that support application functionality.

These may include infrastructure providers, cloud storage, communication providers, notification services, analytics services, or other software integrations.

The identity of relevant providers and their data-handling practices should be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Third-party services may maintain their own privacy notices.

12.Data Storage and Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Depending on the implemented infrastructure, these safeguards may include:

  • Encrypted network communications
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Restricted access to sensitive information
  • Secure credential management
  • Logging and security monitoring
  • Infrastructure access restrictions
  • Security maintenance and vulnerability remediation

No internet service or data-storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to use strong authentication credentials and protect access to their accounts.

13.Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable laws.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, why it was collected, operational needs, and legal obligations.

Certain information may be retained when legally justified, including for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance.

Where such retention applies, the information will be handled according to applicable restrictions and retention policies.

Users must be provided with a clear process to request account deletion and associated personal data removal.

16.Legal Bases for Processing

Where data protection legislation requires a legal basis, Verity will process personal information under an applicable basis, which may include:

  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate interests, where permitted
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Consent, where required
  • Other legally recognized grounds where applicable

The specific basis depends on the processing activity, the jurisdiction, and the nature of the information.

Where special-category or sensitive information is processed, additional legal conditions may apply.

17.Children's Privacy

Verity is committed to respecting the privacy of children and young people.

The minimum permitted age for creating an account will be stated in Verity's Terms of Service and enforced according to the application's verified age-eligibility policies and applicable laws.

If we become aware that personal information was collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue, including deletion where required.

Parents or guardians with concerns may contact the privacy team.

18.International Data Transfers

Verity may use infrastructure or service providers located outside the country where a user resides.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will apply safeguards required by relevant data protection laws.

Users may contact us for additional information about applicable international transfer arrangements.

19.Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

Verity's website and application may use cookies, application identifiers, local storage, analytics tools, and related technologies.

These technologies may support:

  • Authentication
  • Session management
  • User preferences
  • Security
  • Performance monitoring
  • Service analytics
  • Personalization, where implemented

Where legally required, we will obtain consent before activating nonessential tracking technologies and provide appropriate controls.

  • Account security

Users may manage notification permissions through their device settings and available Verity preferences.
